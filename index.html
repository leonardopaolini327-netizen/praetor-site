Don't take our word for it. Check the record yourself.

Each hourly analysis is added to a hash chain and anchored in the Bitcoin blockchain. After that, no one can edit or delete a result without it showing, including us.

  1. 1Download any analysis and its .ots proof from the public record.
  2. 2Drop both files on opentimestamps.org.
  3. 3Read which Bitcoin block recorded it, and when.

The public record opens at launch. Until then, write to support@praetortrading.finance and we'll send you any record with its proof.

Who's behind Praetor

Praetor is built by Leonardo Paolini, an independent web developer in Busto Arsizio, Italy. It's a one-person project, and that shapes how it works: instead of asking you to trust a person, Praetor is designed to be checked.

Five models from five different companies have to agree before a signal goes out. Every analysis, including the losing trades and the quiet hours, is recorded and timestamped on Bitcoin. If the numbers aren't good enough, you'll see that here too.

Questions

When does Praetor launch?

Praetor launches on January 1, 2027 at 12:00 CET. Until then it is being tested with paper trading. Join the waitlist and we'll email you when it opens.

Do I need trading experience?

No. You choose your markets, receive signals and decide whether to accept them. The onboarding starts with a practice trade using play money.

Are these real-money results?

No. The figures on this page come from paper trading: real market prices, simulated money.

Why are the figures 24 hours old?

So that the signals stay useful to the people who pay for them. The full record, with nothing removed, is still public after the delay.

Can I stop Praetor at any time?

Yes. You can pause signals whenever you like, or stop Praetor completely. Stopping closes any open trades.

How do I know results haven't been edited?

Every analysis is anchored in the Bitcoin blockchain, so it can be checked independently on opentimestamps.org.

Is this financial advice?

No. Signals are generated by AI models and are not personalised financial advice. Every trade is your decision.
