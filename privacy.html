Privacy notice

Website and waitlist · Last updated: 10 November 2026

This notice explains how we handle personal data when you visit praetortrading.finance or join the Praetor waitlist. Praetor is not open yet. A full Privacy Policy covering the Praetor app will be published before launch.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Leonardo Paolini, operating Praetor Trading from Busto Arsizio (VA), Italy. Contact: support@praetortrading.finance.

What we collect

  • Waitlist: your email address, your country, your trading experience, how you heard about Praetor, the fact that you gave consent, and the date you signed up.
  • Website visits: our hosting provider automatically processes technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the page and keep it secure.

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and fonts are served from our own site.

Why we use it

  • To email you when Praetor launches and, occasionally, with updates about the testing. Legal basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • To understand where interest comes from (countries, experience level, how people found us), so we can decide where to launch first. Legal basis: your consent, given when you join the waitlist.
  • To deliver and secure the website. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a safe website.

Who processes it for us

  • GitHub (Microsoft), which hosts this website. Data may be processed in the United States under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Brevo (France), which stores the waitlist and sends our emails. Data is processed in the European Union.

We do not sell your personal data or share it with anyone else.

How long we keep it

We keep your email until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it, and in any case no longer than 12 months after Praetor launches unless you create an account.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, withdraw consent, or object to its use, by writing to support@praetortrading.finance. Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it); in the UK, the ICO (ico.org.uk); elsewhere, the authority where you live.

Age

The waitlist and Praetor itself are only for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

If we change this notice, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Risk warning: trading involves significant risk and you may lose some or all of the capital you invest. Signals are generated by artificial intelligence models and do not constitute personalised financial advice.
